## 今日趋势观察

1. Agents are receiving product-grade communication surfaces: Sentio gives agents inboxes, Wbrowser/Huashu give them existing Chrome state, and swissdevjobs-cli gives them a structured job-search command surface.
2. Completion evidence is becoming its own product category: task-state-guard, AgentSeed, visual-diff, Consistorium and FlowCommit all try to turn agent claims into auditable observations or transactions.
3. Security and compliance workflows are moving from passive scans to agent-triggered action: Patchbot and FlowCommit both separate scanning/intent from verified effects and approvals.
4. Vertical agent UX is becoming more concrete: video RAG, voice-agent PWAs, Koha library assistants, photography copilots and job boards show agents attached to domain artifacts rather than generic chat.
5. Fresh search results still contain many skill catalogs, course repos and generated shells; Docker/CLI/API/MCP docs, screenshots, demo GIFs, CI badges and explicit failure boundaries were the strongest filters today.
