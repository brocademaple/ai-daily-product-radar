## 今日趋势观察

The strongest projects in today's window are operating layers around agents: voice workspaces that hand artifacts between coding agents, visual blueprint compilers for multiple harnesses, cost ledgers, readback verifiers, typed browser APIs, and context-continuity services. Product-shaped vertical agents are also getting more explicit about consent and approvals, especially in benefits renewal, procurement, and voice support. The weak side remains familiar: many repositories use agent language for personal profiles, tutorials, unsafe account automation, pentesting, messaging automation, or regulation/finance workflows without enough independent runtime and safety evidence.
