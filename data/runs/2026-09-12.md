## 今日趋势观察

Today is unusually strong for agent-control infrastructure. The best projects are not generic chatbots: they wrap agents in rollback, least-privilege, fail-closed, authority-resolution, browser-reality, real-document, Android-accessibility, or voice-diagnostic loops. MCP keeps spreading into niche personal data and creative workflows, while job-search and contractor voice agents are adding explicit human checkpoints. The weak side of the window is familiar: many repos use agent language for portfolios, educational exercises, trading/crypto workbenches, or safety-sensitive automation without enough consent and verification structure.
