## 今日趋势观察

AI-native product energy continues to move from single assistant demos into operational surfaces around agents: terminal multiplexers, desktop control planes, persistent memory, session browsers, and browser/component frameworks. The strongest products are making agent activity observable and resumable rather than just adding a chat box. Computer-use is also splitting into platform-specific control layers: Windows MCP, signed-in browser control, macOS/iPhone bridges, and browser-native component agents. The radar should keep rewarding projects that expose permissions, run logs, worktree/session boundaries, and human review points.
