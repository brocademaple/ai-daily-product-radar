## 今日趋势观察

Today's strongest signal is the migration of AI-native tooling into local control surfaces: macOS accessibility computer-use, text-first desktop control, Visual Studio/Copilot orchestration, browser-test generation, and documentation drift repair. Jev/Laya-style typed decisions are becoming a reusable primitive for browser fields, test selection, and form filling, but the radar should keep separating complete workflow products from thin model demos. Vertical RAG continues to look credible when it ships evidence views, sanitized sample data, or concrete domain routing rather than another generic chat shell.
