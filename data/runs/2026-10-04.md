## 今日趋势观察

Today the strongest AI-native product signal is agent operations becoming visible and stateful: multi-agent teams, cross-agent model routers, local memory stores, token/context middleware, visual artifact canvases, remote sandboxes, approval cards, and bug queues that agents can claim. The product frontier is less about another chatbot shell and more about durable surfaces around agency: roles, permissions, receipts, context ownership, schedules, and human review points.
