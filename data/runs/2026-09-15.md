## 今日趋势观察

The strongest new signals are not generic chat apps: they are control surfaces around agent work. Local operator consoles, evidence-gated task boards, policy compilers, side-effect receipts, shared memory stores, and bounded workstation MCP servers are turning agent runs into auditable operations. Vertical products still matter when the workflow is concrete, but repos with account automation, free-provider claims, reverse-engineering, or regulatory/customer-contact actions need much stronger permission and safety evidence before promotion.
