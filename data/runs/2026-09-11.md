## 今日趋势观察

Today is a small-new-product day rather than a mega-star day. The strongest pattern is operational infrastructure around coding agents: phone supervision, queue control, read-only GitHub context gateways, cross-agent session handoff, provenance-aware version control, and secret redaction. Agent video and demo tooling also moved from prompt assets toward MCP/CLI pipelines that can record, render, and review artifacts. Vertical agents are getting more serious about receipts and human gates, especially job-search and contact-center workflows. Several physical-world, finance, and protocol projects are intriguing but remain below Top until code, permissions, and safety boundaries are clearer.
