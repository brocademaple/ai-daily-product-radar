## 今日趋势观察

1. Coding-agent control planes are turning into full products: Orca, ADHDev, Ronin and Take Ur Turn all assume multiple agents, parallel worktrees and human approval/review as normal product primitives.
2. Context and proof are moving closer to the agent runtime: Graft optimizes repository context, Perenna/Take Ur Turn persist memory, and Goal to Proof pushes final evidence into the agent’s work contract.
3. Permission and credential boundaries are becoming more concrete: Cover, Faramir and PATless all work at the privacy/secret/identity boundary instead of relying only on policy text.
4. Vertical agent studios are getting more specific: LiveStream Agent Studio and YoloCut show agent UX moving from generic chat to operator workflows with domain artifacts.
5. Fresh repos still contain many skill catalogs and generated READMEs; install paths, screenshots, API/MCP surfaces, approval mechanics and threat models were the strongest filters today.
